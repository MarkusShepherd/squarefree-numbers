\begin{abstract}
For a polynomial $f\in\Z[t]$ of degree $d\ge1$ with $f(t)\ge2$ and $f'(t)\ge1$ for $t\ge1$, we study the weighted variance
\[
V_f(x,y)=\sum_{k\le y}f'(k)\sum_{l=1}^{f(k)}\Bigl(Q(x;f(k),l)-g(f(k),l)\,x\Bigr)^2
\]
of the number $Q(x;q,l)$ of squarefree $n\le x$ in the residue class $l\bmod q$, where the moduli are restricted to values of $f$ and every residue class is included. We prove that, uniformly for $f(y)\le x$,
\[
V_f(x,y)=x^2F_f\bigl(x/f(y)\bigr)+O_{f,\eps}\bigl(x^{2-\eta_d+\eps}\bigr),\qquad \eta_d=\min\Bigl\{\frac15,\frac1{4d-1}\Bigr\},
\]
with an explicit profile $F_f$ satisfying $F_f(H)=K_fH^{-3/2}+O(H^{-13/8})$, and hence
$V_f(x,y)\sim K_fx^{1/2}f(y)^{3/2}$ for $x^{1-2\eta_d/3+\delta}\le f(y)\le x^{1-\delta}$. The constant $K_f>0$ is $-\frac83\zeta(-\frac12)$ times an absolutely convergent Euler product built from the numbers of roots of $f$ modulo $p$ and $p^2$; no irreducibility or squarefreeness assumption on $f$ is needed. The proof combines an elementary uniform estimate for correlations of squarefree numbers, an average of the resulting singular series over polynomial moduli, and a Mellin-transform analysis of a Dirichlet series whose pole at $s=-\frac12$ produces the main term. We compare the predictions with exact computations of $V_f$ up to $x=2.56\cdot10^8$ for $f(t)=t^2+1$, $t^3+2$ and $2t+1$.
\end{abstract}
