(1) Exactness: all counts (sum_l Q^2, sum_l g Q numerators, Q(x)) are exact integers; the combination T - 2uM/q' + u^2 S is evaluated in double-double arithmetic (~1e-30 relative on the terms, which are ~x^2/q while V_q ~ x; i.e. V is good to >= 15 digits). "V" in rows is a float; "V_exact" is the decimal string (hi+lo), only the first ~25 digits are meaningful.
(2) g(q,l) = (6/pi^2) * prod_{p|q} p^2/(p^2-1) / q * prod_{p||q, p|l} (1-1/p) * [no prime p with p^2 | gcd(q,l)].  For squarefree n the last bracket is 1, so sum_l g Q only needs the factor prod_{p||q,p|n}(1-1/p).
(3) y is chosen as the integer nearest to f^{-1}(ratio*x) subject to f(y)<=x, so the actual ratio fy/x differs from the nominal one (see ratio_actual in "normalised"; for k^3+2 at small x the quantisation is several %). "V_over_x12_fy32" is at the actual (y,fy); "V_over_x12_fy32_at_nominal_ratio" linearly interpolates N=V/(x^(1/2) fy^(3/2)) in fy between adjacent y to the exact nominal ratio. Stability test and extrapolation use the nominal-ratio values.
(4) THE NORMALISED VALUE IS STABLE IN x BUT NOT FLAT IN THE RATIO r=f(y)/x, AND NOT SMOOTH IN r. N(r) increases from ~0.144 (r=0.01) to ~0.171-0.184 (r=1) for all three f. A dense scan (see constant_extrapolated.dense_grid_check; linear case at x=5e6) shows N(r) has kinks/oscillations of size ~0.005-0.02 at r=1/2,1/3,1/4,1/5,... (presumably from floor(x/q), the number of multiples j*q <= x, in sum_l Q^2 = Q(x)+2*sum_j C(jq)). The grid ratios 0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1,0.2 are all of the form 1/m, so they sit on such features and low-order polynomial/sqrt fits through the 8 grid points are model dependent. Hence the fixed-x exponent beta is ~1.54-1.55, not 1.5: the 0.04 excess is just d log N / d log r over r in [0.01,1] and is NOT an asymptotic exponent. At fixed ratio, alpha+beta = 2.000 (linear 2.00012+-0.00006, k^2+1 1.9998+-0.0007; k^3+2 1.988+-0.005 not yet converged because its y are small, y<=215, and the arithmetic fluctuation of N is ~1-2%).
(5) Stability in x at fixed ratio (two largest x): linear 2e6->5e6: all 8 ratios change < 0.1% (r=0.01: 0.1%); k^2+1 5e6->1e7: all < 0.4%; k^3+2 1e7 vs 5e6: ratios 0.01..0.1 change 0.5-2.2% (not stable), >=0.2 change <0.4%. Earlier x show small-ratio values converge from BELOW for the linear case (r=0.01: 0.129 at x=1e4, 0.1446 at 5e6) and with a slow, noisy drift for k^2+1 and k^3+2 (values at r<=0.05 move by ~1-3% between x=1e5 and 1e7).
(6) Extrapolated constant (r->0) is ~0.14 for all three f, with total uncertainty ~0.01 (~7%); polynomial independence is suggested but not established at this precision. Different fit forms ([1,r], [1,r,r^2], [1,sqrt r], [1,sqrt r,r]) give values from 0.135 to 0.15; the reported value uses the form with smallest leave-one-out error on the grid rows above the stability cutoff and is therefore not much better than the model spread. The dense-grid cross-check (windows [0.01..0.05, 0.1..0.2]) gives medians 0.141 (k^2+1), 0.143 (k^3+2), 0.146 (2k+1) with window/model range 0.127-0.151 (the extremes come from 3-parameter fits on short windows).  A prover's predicted constant should be compared to N(r) at r=1/2..1, where numerics are sharp (3-4 digits): N(1.0) = 0.1712 (k^2+1, x=1e7), 0.1680 (k^3+2, x=1e7, still drifting down: 0.1696, 0.1685, 0.1680 at x=2e6, 5e6, 1e7; use with care), 0.1837 (2k+1, x=5e6).
