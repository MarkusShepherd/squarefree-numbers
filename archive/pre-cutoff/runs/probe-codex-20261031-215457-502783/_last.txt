The related result I can identify confidently is the classical **Barban–Davenport–Halberstam theorem** for primes, in its von Mangoldt form. With
\[
\psi(x;q,a)=\sum_{\substack{n\le x\\n\equiv a\pmod q}}\Lambda(n),
\]
one standard bound is
\[
\sum_{q\le Q}\ \sum_{\substack{a\bmod q\\(a,q)=1}}
\left|\psi(x;q,a)-\frac{\psi(x)}{\varphi(q)}\right|^2
\ll xQ\log x
\qquad (Q\le x).
\]
This is an all-moduli mean-square bound; it is not a theorem about the sparse polynomial moduli \(q=f(k)\), and it does not give an asymptotic with a leading constant as stated here. **Confidence: high** on the bound and its classical attribution to Barban and Davenport–Halberstam; I’m not supplying a sharper asymptotic or a more precise range from memory.
