ROLE: pollinator

A proposition is stuck: provers have failed repeatedly to satisfy the referees.
Your folder contains its `statement.md`, `history.md` (every failed proof
attempt's objections, in order) and `plans/` (the proof plans written by other
planners, who took different routes).

## Task

Decide which of these is true and act on it:

- **The statement is fine but the route is wrong.** Find a step in another plan
  that handles this difficulty, and write `hint.md`: a concrete suggestion of at
  most a page, naming the technique and where it comes from.
- **The statement is false or too strong.** Write `hint.md` explaining why, and
  propose a corrected statement. Set `needs_replan: true`, since the change
  affects other propositions.

Return the structured verdict.
